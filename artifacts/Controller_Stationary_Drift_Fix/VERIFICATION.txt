ROOT CAUSE
The active controller is Samsung Odyssey/WMR PID 065D. Its IMU fields use signed 24-bit little-endian decoding, as implemented by Monado's WMR protocol helper. The live source decoded the three bytes in reverse order, turning stationary gyro noise into roughly 100 rad/s-class motion. Windows also emits all-zero low-rate idle reports; these must preserve the previous orientation rather than enter AHRS as a fresh acceleration sample. The fix restores little-endian signed decoding, holds orientation on idle frames, rejects implausible non-idle frames, qualifies gyro bias using near-gravity acceleration, and zeros corrected sub-0.015 rad/s stationary noise.
